Meet Fiona

Having worked successfully for over 35 years in a variety of highly sensitive front facing roles, I bring professionalism to my work with funeral directors and compassion, support and care to bereaved families.

My own experiences of loss have given me a personal understanding of grief. If you entrust something this important to me, I will consider it a privilege to be handled with the utmost care, honesty, and respect.

"I am fully trained and insured by The Academy of Professional Celebrants and I hold a certificate of distinction in Funeral Celebrancy."

Services

Funeral Ceremony

I meet with you to understand your wishes, liaise with your funeral director and write a bespoke ceremony that captures the essence of your loved one.

Memorial Service

Following a direct cremation or private funeral, I design a tailored ceremony to reflect and celebrate your loved one’s life and legacy.

Burial or Scattering of Ashes

I write and deliver a bespoke ceremony to mark this act of special significance, ensuring the moment is handled with dignity.

Pet Committal or Memorial

Pets are members of the family. I design tailored ceremonies honouring the passing of your beloved friend and companion.
